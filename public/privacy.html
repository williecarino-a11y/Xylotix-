Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Miimiid ("we", "our", "the app") is a financial literacy app. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (if you sign up with email).
  • Date of birth: used to tailor learning content to your age group.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • App activity: your progress, scores, and messages you send to the AI Tutor.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save your learning progress and personalise your experience.
  • To provide the AI Tutor and other features of the app.
  • To improve and fix problems in the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run the app (for example, hosting and AI services), and only as needed to provide the service, or if required by law.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Passwords are not stored in plain text. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Miimiid is intended for learners. If you are under 13, please use the app only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your account and data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Changes
